NOW Methyl B12 (Lozenges / Veg Capsules)

What is NOW Methyl B-12?

Vitamin B-12 is necessary for the production of energy from fats and proteins and is well known for its critical role in DNA synthesis, as well as in homocysteine metabolism. † Methylcobalamin is the co-enzyme form of vitamin B-12 that has been found to be especially critical in the maintenance of a healthy nervous system. † Dietary sources are almost exclusively of animal origin (meat and dairy) and supplementation with B-12 may be especially important for strict vegetarians. NOW® Methyl B-12 is vegetarian/vegan (non-animal derived).

From the FDA: "As part of a well-balanced diet that is low in saturated fat and cholesterol, Vitamin B12 may reduce the risk of vascular disease. FDA evaluated the above claim and found that, while it is known that diets low in saturated fat and cholesterol reduce the risk of heart disease and other vascular diseases, the evidence in support of the above claim is inconclusive."

How do I take NOW Methyl B-12?

Dosages and Directions:

Dosages vary on the potency of the product.

  • 1,000 mcg Lozenges: Take 1 lozenge 1 to 2 times daily. Chew lozenge or hold in mouth until dissolved and swallow.
  • 5,000 mcg Lozenges: Take 1 lozenge daily with a meal. Chew lozenge or hold in mouth until dissolved and swallow.
  • 5,000 mcg Veg Capsules: Take 1 capsule daily.
  • 10,000 mcg Lozenges: Take 1 lozenge daily. Chew lozenge or hold in mouth until dissolved and swallow.
